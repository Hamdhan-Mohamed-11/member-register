## Brand & Style

The design system evokes the thoughtful warmth of a storied independent bookstore blended with the crisp curation of an exclusive cultural reading society. It rejects sterile SaaS motifs and high-friction transactional layouts in favor of an inviting, editorial aesthetic. 

The aesthetic is grounded in classical modernism: generous breathing room, refined bookish typography, tactile paper-like surface contrasts, and deliberate typographic flourishes. The tone conveys authority, literary passion, intellectual warmth, and community intimacy. High-contrast typography pairs with quiet structured cards, creating an intuitive reading environment that honors both mobile utility and literary tradition.

## Colors

The palette balances authoritative naval blues with the tactile warmth of physical print.

- **Primary Navy (`#293896`)**: Anchors critical actions, section titles, and key brand markers. Deeper tones (`#16205C`, `#0F1640`) provide heavyweight anchors for headers and footers, while `#EFF2FB` provides soft active container tints.
- **Brand Sky (`#00AEEF`)**: Reserved for digital highlights, category tags, and interactive links. Always substitute with `#0079A8` whenever placed as body or metadata text over light backdrops to meet AA contrast requirements.
- **Literary Gold (`#F9CD59`)**: Applied strictly as an accent—such as member tiers, bookmarks, awards, and reading milestones. When rendered as text on light surfaces, use `#8A6410`.
- **Surfaces**: The primary canvas relies on `#F8F9FC` with `#FFFFFF` for primary cards. The warm cream panel `#F8F2E9` introduces an archival, book-page richness for featured reviews, editorial notes, and spotlight chapters.
- **Status Tokens**: Soft pastels with deep chromatic inks ensure alerts do not disrupt the literary tone.

## Typography

The type system creates an intentional dialogue between the elegance of physical publishing and legible digital ergonomics:

- **Headings (Playfair Display)**: Used for storytelling, section titles, and pull quotes. Feature italic variants on primary key nouns or phrases to introduce an authentic bookshop flourish (e.g., *Book of the Month*, *The Salon*).
- **Body & Interface (DM Sans)**: Provides high-clarity neutral balance to complement Playfair Display's serif drama, maintaining legibility at scale on compact screens.
- **Formatting Conventions**:
  - **Currency**: Render prices using the formal code and non-breaking space: `LKR 4,750`. Numeric weights stay medium/semi-bold.
  - **Dates**: Standardize across all UI to the British literary convention without ordinal suffixes: `1 Mar 2028`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout hierarchy follows a mobile-first 360px viewport baseline, expanding gracefully into multi-column tablet and desktop experiences.

- **Grid Architecture**:
  - **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `16px` outer margins and `16px` gutters. Full edge-to-edge card strips allow smooth horizontal scrolling for featured titles.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout with `24px` margins and `20px` gutters.
  - **Desktop (>1024px)**: 12-column layout maxing out at `1200px` content width with `40px` margins and `24px` gutters.
- **Spacing Rhythm**: Internal component padding follows an 8pt architectural step. Editorial breathing room takes precedence over hyper-dense packing—card containers always feature at least `16px` to `24px` internal padding to preserve readability.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through tactile surface separation, fine boundaries, and diffused ambient illumination rather than harsh drop-shadows:

- **Tonal Layers**: The foundational canvas rests on `#F8F9FC`. Interactive modules, book entries, and reading logs elevate through `#FFFFFF` fills backed by a fine line border (`#E6E9F0`).
- **Warm Editorial Panels**: `#F8F2E9` panel containers introduce warm, book-page depth without cast shadows, acting as organic anchors for curated selections.
- **Ambient Shadow System**:
  - **Resting Cards**: `0px 2px 8px rgba(22, 32, 92, 0.04), 0px 1px 2px rgba(22, 32, 92, 0.02)`
  - **Elevated/Hover**: `0px 8px 24px rgba(22, 32, 92, 0.07), 0px 2px 6px rgba(22, 32, 92, 0.04)`
  - **Overlays/Drawers**: `0px 16px 36px rgba(15, 22, 64, 0.12)`

## Shapes

The geometric silhouette balances approachable organic warmth with modern structural stability:

- **Standard Cards**: Standardized at an exact `14px` border radius (`rounded-lg` override) to emulate smooth book jackets and stationery cards.
- **Large Panels & Dialogs**: `24px` radius on hero highlights, curator spotlights, modal sheets, and warm cream accent containers.
- **Interactive Elements**: Input fields, search bars, and utility controls use `8px` corner radius. Buttons use `8px` or pill forms (`9999px`) exclusively for lightweight category tags.
- **Visual Iconography**: Line-drawn icons strictly at `1.6px` stroke width with rounded join caps. No emojis are permitted anywhere in structural or display UI.

## Components

- **Buttons**:
  - *Primary*: `#293896` solid background with white text, `8px` radius, subtle `0 2px 4px rgba(41, 56, 150, 0.2)` shadow. Hover deepens to `#16205C`.
  - *Secondary*: Transparent background with `1.5px` border in `#293896`, text in `#293896`. Hover fills with `#EFF2FB`.
  - *Tertiary/Ghost*: Transparent background with `#0079A8` or `#293896` text, zero border, active background `#EFF2FB`.
- **Chips & Badges**:
  - *Genre/Filter Chips*: Default `#FFFFFF` with `#E6E9F0` border and `#5A6178` text. Selected state switches to `#EFF2FB` surface with `#293896` outline and text.
  - *Status Badges*: Pill-shaped (`9999px`), `4px 10px` padding with corresponding muted background and high-contrast foreground text (e.g., `#DCF3E4` container with `#147233` label).
- **Cards**:
  - *Book Listing Card*: `#FFFFFF` surface, `14px` radius, `1px solid #E6E9F0`. Features book cover aspect ratio (2:3) with a hairline inner ring shadow simulating book spine depth.
  - *Curator Spotlight*: `#F8F2E9` surface, `24px` radius, bordered subtly with `rgba(138, 100, 16, 0.15)`. Uses Playfair Display italic heading.
- **Input Fields**:
  - High-comfort `44px` minimum height, `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E6E9F0`, `8px` corner radius. Focus states shift border to `2px solid #293896` with zero fuzzy outer glow. Placeholder text in `#8B91A3`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**:
  - Square (`6px` radius) and circular controls in `#E6E9F0` unselected state. Selected state transitions to `#293896` with clean white micro check/dot icons.
- **Lists & Book Clubs Index**:
  - Separated by hairline dividers (`#E6E9F0`), each cell containing a thumbnail, title in `#14162E` Playfair, and meeting date meta formatted cleanly (`1 Mar 2028`) in `#5A6178`.
- **Reading Progress Bar**:
  - Track height `6px` in `#EFF2FB`, active fill in `#293896`, milestone points accented with `#F9CD59`.
