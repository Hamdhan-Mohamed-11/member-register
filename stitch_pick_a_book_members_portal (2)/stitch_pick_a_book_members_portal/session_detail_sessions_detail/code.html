About this session

In this deep-dive salon, we unpack Shehan Karunatilaka’s Booker Prize-winning tour de force, The Seven Moons of Maali Almeida. Set against the turmoil and fractures of 1989 Colombo, the novel transforms a murder mystery into a dazzling metaphysical satire, guiding readers through the bureaucratic purgatory of the afterlife with razor-sharp wit and poignant grief.

Our discussion centers on the intersection of war photojournalism and magical realism, asking how humor preserves memory in the face of political erasure. Members are encouraged to bring flagged excerpts on photographic memory, spirit borders, and the moral weight carried by Maali’s contact sheets.
