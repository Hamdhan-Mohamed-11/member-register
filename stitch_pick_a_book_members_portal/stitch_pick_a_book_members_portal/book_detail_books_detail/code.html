Editorial Synopsis

Colombo, 1989. Maali Almeida, war photographer, gambler and closet queen, has woken up dead in what seems like a celestial visa office. His dismembered body is sinking in the Beira Lake and he has no idea who killed him. In a nation where scores are settled by death squads and suicide bombers, the list of suspects is depressingly long. But before time runs out, Maali has seven moons to contact the man and woman he loves most and lead them to the photos that will rock Sri Lanka.
